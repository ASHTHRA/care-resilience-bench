THE GAP

FHIR-valid can still be workflow-unsafe.

CareResilience Bench focuses on behavior under failure: whether a client detects incomplete records, handles authorization expiry, contains duplicate data, warns on stale clinical information, and recovers from transient outages without silently misrepresenting the patient record.
